What it solves

The same real-time voice capability, built three times under three different host relationships: you own it, it calls out to a platform, and it is called by a host.

Not another voice demo — end-of-turn detection, interruption, speaking while receiving, filling silence while the platform is slow, and false-claim detection are all first-class concerns, each backed by a re-runnable probe or unit test.

Three projects · two opposite directions
